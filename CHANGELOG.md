# Changelog

What changed in each version. The release workflow publishes a version's section as its release notes, and installed copies show them when an update arrives.

## Unreleased

- **Slayer: sporali count toward Plants Concrete and Stone.** eqlwiki calls Runnyeye's sporali, Guk's fungusmen and Innothule's fungus spores "Fungusman", while the achievement lists Sporalis, so their kills counted toward nothing between exports and the plan never sent you to them. They now count as Sporali, and Plan › Slayer offers their camps: Runnyeye's sporali, Guk's fungusmen, and Innothule's fungus spores, which the spawn tables make the quickest. That fungusmen count the same as sporali is a guess from eqlwiki's guide; your next achievements export will show whether they do.
- **Motes: buff and debuff ranks grow, and Next is now Best upgrade.** A rank now adds to a buff's or debuff's effects, as spell windows show since the change: max HP 10%, attack and healing 3%, haste 2.5%, stats, resists and AC 2% (given or taken), and a proc a rank of its own every two. Slows, movement and endurance regen don't grow. Spell upgrades counts this, and **Best upgrade** (the Next tab, renamed) ranks a buff's next rank beside your gear merges: its stats by your Gear weights, as though you keep the buff up, and a proc buff by the proc's share of your damage.
- **Factions: the newer zones' own factions get a standing.** The game leaves factions such as Host of Nektropos, Nerius Trade Association and Dread Slayers out of its faction window and the `/outputfile faction` export, so Standings showed only their net change. A faction your logs name that the export leaves out now starts at 0 (Indifferent) and adds every change the log saw, or sits at 2000 or −2000 where the game said it could get no better or worse. Its con word is that standing alone, since the game's modifier table doesn't list these factions.

## 2.20.0 (2026-10-26)

- **Your mana cue waits for Cannibalization.** With **Only while Cannibalization is ready** on (Triggers, under "Say when your own mana is low"), your mana is not read for Cannibalization's recast after you use it, three minutes unless you change it. When it is ready again, mana still under your line is said again, even if it never came back over. The game's own "You can use the ability Cannibalization again in…" sets the time left. Turn it off and your mana is read all the time, as before.
- **The Zookeeper counts aviaks, gorillas and pegasi.** Its list names Birds, Apes and Mystical Horses, which are groups, not races, so none of those mobs counted toward it between exports and the Slayer plan never sent you to their camps. Each now counts as its own achievement lists it: Featherbrained Plan's birds, Better Get a Barrel's apes and Slayer of Mystical Horses' horses. Sea Mammals has no list in the game's files and is still read as written.
- **Gear › Focus effects: Mana Preservation III is a rank of Mana Preservation, not a separate row.** From III on, the focus skips spells with a percent heal, and that one extra limit made it a separate "Mana cost" row. It is now the top rank of one row, with II in its **Enough** list. The same fix puts Naki's Pernicity in Spell Haste's casting-speed row. What each rank is worth is still worked out from its own limits.
- **Respawns: how many times you've killed each mob.** A new **Kill counts** tab on the Respawns page lists every mob your logs saw die, zone by zone, with a zone's instances counted together. For example, The Game Master: 19 in Nektropos Castle. The counts cover the live log, its archives and your other PCs' logs of the character. You can filter by mob or zone, sort by any column, and pick another character.
- **Gear eqlwiki doesn't have yet, from eqlegendstools.com.** eqlwiki has no pages for Nektropos Castle's drops, so the Gear tools didn't know them. The tracker now reads eqlegendstools.com's pages for any gear missing from eqlwiki's catalog and adds those items to the catalog, with eqlwiki's own page winning once it has one. Gear you wear that eqlwiki lacks now shows its stats on the Gear page. The pages are read again weekly when a Gear tool opens, and the item links go to that site. Nektropos Castle counts as in era. Settings › Data sources has a row for it.
- **Pet gear follows the October pet patches.** A pet weapon is now judged on its ratio alone, as the 10-13 and 10-22 patches have pets do: one with a better ratio than the pet's own is used, and a worse one no longer counts for its higher damage. Since 10-22 a summoned pet's HP depends on its STA and its mana on WIS and INT both, so a pet whose wiki page lists spells now values mana, WIS and INT. One that casts nothing still gives them no weight. How much a point buys a pet is not published, so the planner uses what it would buy a player of the pet's classes. Pet pages are read again from the wiki once, to see which pets cast.
- **Gear: what you loot counts before the next export.** The upgrade finder, the focus and effects tabs and the optimizer now treat gear your log saw you keep on looting since your inventory export as yours. The optimizer's lock picker lists those pieces under **Looted since the export**. A merge on looting ("to create an Indicolite Bracer +6") raises the copy it most likely went into. The log does not say what you sold or destroyed afterwards, so a notice above the tools says how many pieces are counted this way, and the next `/outputfile inventory` sets it straight. Gear auto-loot sold or stored in the Dragon Hoard is left out, as the export leaves it out.
- **Your mana and group health read JBUI's bars.** Both read nothing since the skin's bars began shading from dark at the left to bright at the right: a bar is now its colour at any brightness, and its empty part anything as dark as the empty colour or darker. Seen on the real Player window (the cue read 87% where the game said 87%); the Group window's rows are read the same way but are not yet tried in a group.
- **Slayer: a plan of pet kills alone.** **Pet kills only** at the top of Plan › Slayer plans for your pet to farm alone: every camp keeps only the mobs that con green or lower to you (level 32 and under at 50, as the classic server cons, and your own /con lines agree), the raid lockouts drop out, and a camp's pace is what is left. A mob's level comes from the spawn tables, else from your own /con lines (so the Plane of Sky's soul tamers, 56, are out and New Sebilis Expedition's golems, 14 to 16, stay); a mob neither knows stays in. Kept for each character. Your logs are read once more after the update, for the levels.
- **Slayer: a camp is where a pet farms, not a whole zone.** Where the plan paces a zone by its spawn tables, it now counts only what pops within a pet's reach of the best place to stand, on one floor, instead of every spot in the zone. A zone with two good places for different achievements is two camps, the second named by its /waypoint. Mobs more than five levels over your character are left out of these camps, since a pet left alone loses to them. A zone you have hunted no longer hides a better place in it: where a pet's spot there clearly beats your log's pace for an achievement, both are offered (Lesser Faydark's faeries beside its brownies). Your Conquest plan now sends you to the Qeynos Aqueducts for Swordfishmermaid and the Lesser Faydark faeries for Legendary Creatures, and no longer to Cazic-Thule's oozes for A Sight for Sore Eyes.

## 2.19.0 (2026-10-20)

- **Two PCs at once: pick which one Live follows.** While this PC's game is closed and two or more other PCs' logs are being written, the Live page's "Following … on another PC" notice has a **Follow** list: **Automatic** (the newest, this PC's character first, as before) or any log written in the last quarter hour. A log picked is followed at once and kept until it goes quiet a quarter hour or Automatic is picked again; this PC's log still comes first whenever its game is played.
- **Live: Hunter and Slayer cards.** A hunt running here or on another PC shows on Live: the Hunter named the zone still wants, the ones killed since your export and when, one killed in the last half hour called out, and the Slayer counts the kills since the export moved, the latest first. Each Hunter named a kill on any of your logs ticks off is written to the feed and, with the achievement cues on, flashed on the overlay and said ("Hunter named down: a goblin alchemist").
- **Hunter: a Windows notification when a wanted named dies.** So a hunt left running on another PC is heard of away from the game; clicking it brings the window up. **Notify me** on Live's Hunter card turns it off.
- **Spell Quests, a module of its own.** Settings › Modules turns on **Spell Quests** under Goals: Tashania, Ice Comet, Divine Might, Life Leech and Call of Flame, each a checklist like an epic's, kept from its eqlwiki quest page; the scroll in hand covers the quest. Epics and spell quests count together, so an item both want (five Enchanted Platinum Bars) is wanted by both, on Targets and in the drop cue.
- **Epics and Spell Quests: tick the classes to show.** A box for each class, kept for each character, with **Yours**, **All** and **None**; your own classes stand out.
- **Epics: "Wanted here".** The pieces your classes still need from the zone you are in show on the achievements overlay as **Wanted here** (its own switch), and zoning in says them on the alert overlay ("Epic pieces here: Ghoulbane (Shadow Knight)"), not again for ten minutes in the same zone.
- **Slayer: Every day's lockout kills.** A raid lockout's kills today show as a box a difficulty beside its mobs, ticked as your log sees them die, green when the day's are done. The day starts at the game's daily reset, noon US Eastern, not local midnight, and the count comes from the log, so exporting achievements after a run no longer empties its boxes.
- **Respawns: a spawn point pinned to a /loc.** For a name that spawns in more than one place, a spawn point can carry its spot (your /loc there, and how near): a kill of that name followed within 30 seconds by a /loc in range is the spot's, and its timer runs from that death. The name keeps its own records for every other kill in the zone.
- **Motes › Next sorts.** Each column sorts the list (click again to reverse; remembered), and a **Per 1,000** column shows the gain for each 1,000 motes spent, the list's own order.
- **Gear: maxed items in violet.** An item merged as far as it goes shows its +N and pips in violet, and the character card shows Merged, Average and At +10 as three numbers over the total merge progress bar.
- **Settings › UI.** A tab for your UI skins: each skin that offers it has its rebuild button here (run after a game patch; moved from General) and, when its legends-tracker.json names one, a command to run each time the game starts, which runs only once you switch it on, with the whole command in view.
- **Icons follow your UI skin.** Spell and item icons come from the skin your character plays with when it carries its own icon sheets, as the game draws them, and from the game's own otherwise; a /loadskin is picked up within a minute.
- **One on/off look.** Every on/off reads as a switch, its knob right and filled when on: settings are switches beside their words, and a filter among others in a row is a chip with a small switch in it, tinted while on. Checkboxes stay for ticking things in a list.
- **Natural-sounding voices, made on your PC.** Settings › Audio's Voice list now has sixteen Kokoro voices below the Windows ones, American and British, women and men (Heart, Emma, George, Michael…). Pick one and its model downloads once (92 MB); after that it works offline, a new phrase takes about a second and is kept, and a timer's cues are ready before they are due. Until the download is done, or if Kokoro ever fails, the Windows voice speaks instead, so no cue is lost.

## 2.18.0 (2026-10-19)

- **Azure voices are gone.** Speech is the Windows voices alone: the Microsoft voices card on Settings › Audio, its key and the phrases it kept are removed. A voice that was an Azure one is now the Windows default; pick another under Voice.

## 2.17.1 (2026-10-19)

- **Epics, an optional module.** Settings › Modules turns it on, and **Epics** joins Goals: what to keep for each class's epic quest, pre-Kunark, as the eqlwiki's "Epic Items To Keep" checklist by Manlaan lists it (the page SEQO reads too), with how many of each you hold from your log and inventory export. A quest's reward in hand covers what makes it (the Darkforge Breastplate its Decayed pieces and bars), the finished epic covers the whole class, an item two of your classes take is wanted by both, and a box on every step ticks off what no file can see. **Targets** says where to hunt for the rest, zone by zone; **Items** is where a count is put right.
- **Epics: a cue when a piece drops.** While the module is on, looting a piece a class on your record still wants, or killing a named that drops one, is said on the Live page's feed and the alert overlay with the class and what it is for ("Ghoulbane dropped: Shadow Knight epic, for the Corrupted Ghoulbane"), as SEQO's toast does; nothing is said of a piece you already hold enough of.

- **The sidebar's groups follow what the pages do.** Four groups where there were three: **Play** (Live, Damage Meter, Loot, Respawns), **Character** (Stats, Gear, Motes, Tradeskills), **Goals** (Achievements, Factions, Slayer, Hunter, and Plane of Sky when it is on) and **Cues** (Spell Timers, Buffs, Triggers: what the app watches for and says). Settings sits at the foot of the sidebar, over who is being watched. Motes moved beside Gear, whose Best merge it shows, and Buffs beside Spell Timers; no page changed inside, and the app opens where you left it.
- **One place to try log lines.** The Triggers page's Test card takes pasted lines: the last is matched against the selected trigger, and **Run for real** runs them all through Spell Timers and every trigger as if just logged; **Try a log line** on Live (in the DoTs & timers card, beside **Show demo timers**) and on Spell Timers opens it. Live's own Try log lines card is gone.
- **One group roster.** The Damage Meter's Group scope and Buffs' Your group show the same roster, and both edit it: add anyone by name, take one off, **Reset group**.
- **Rebuild buttons moved to Settings › General,** beside the game folder, from the head of the Tradeskills page; Bag layout stays on Tradeskills.
- **Slayer's zone respawns are under Assumptions,** with the plan's other inputs and counted in the fold's summary line, where before they had a fold of their own.
- **Plane of Sky: Show ignored instead of an Ignored tab.** A box among the Tests tab's filters draws the ignored Tests faint among the rest, each with **Show it again**; the eighth tab is gone.

## 2.16.0 (2026-10-19)

- **Another PC's game on the Live page.** While this PC's game is closed and its log quiet, a character log in one of Settings › Log files' other PCs' folders that is being written is followed: the Live page, the timers, the meter and the overlays (which show while this window is in front) follow what is played on that PC, the same character's log first. This PC's log is followed again as soon as its game starts or its log is written, or when the other goes quiet for a quarter hour; motes looted there are still counted once. A switch under Other PCs' logs turns it off, and the Live page's notice has **Stop following other PCs**.
- **Mez and charm cues.** A mez or charm is warned of 12 seconds before it ends ("Recast Mesmerize on a gnoll pup"; the time and words are on Spell Timers › Defaults, under Mez and charm announcements), where before only a DoT or a buff on you got a cue. One that ends a tick or more early has broken: "Mesmerize broke on a gnoll pup" is said at once, cutting in.
- **Urgent phrases go first.** A charm breaking and a group member's low health go to the front of the speech queue and are never the phrase dropped when the queue is full; before, every phrase waited its turn behind "Recast Odium" and the oldest waiting was dropped whatever it was.
- **Invisibility bars say "until you act".** An invisibility (Gather Shadows and the like) ends when you act, so its bar shows no clock and cues no recast, and ends on the game's "You feel yourself starting to appear." rather than running its full half hour.
- **Tradeskill achievements count from your skill-ups.** "Reach 150 skill in Baking" and the rest of the Tradeskill sections show the skill as your log last saw it go up against the number asked ("87 / 150"), on the Achievements page, on the overlay's Skills section as it rises, and tracked; the objectives' Smithing and Jewelcrafting are the log's Blacksmithing and Jewelry Making.
- **Conqueror achievements on Hunter.** Raids' "Conqueror of The Plane of Fear" and the rest are on Plan › Hunter, the Slayer steps and the achievements overlay like the Hunter achievements: each boss with its spawn, what your Respawns records say of it ("died 3 days ago"), its map and marks, and **Doesn't work**.
- **Slayer: a step's Hunter named are yours to kill, whoever else killed them.** A named an alt of yours killed after your achievements export was ticked off under the step on the Slayer page while Plan › Hunter still listed it; both now count the character's own kills alone (every character's log still feeds the pace and the travel).
- **Damage meter: active DPS counts the time you strike.** A DoT ticking or a damage shield burning no longer counts as time spent striking, so a caster who lands one DoT and waits shows the active DPS of their casts, and the Procs card's firings a minute are over real striking time.
- **Other PCs' logs moved to Settings › Log files,** beside the character logs, from Settings › General.
- **Buff timers pause while a zone loads.** A buff carried through a zone line runs on by the loading screen's length, as the game's does, so Spirit of the Puma no longer runs out (and cues its recast) a quarter minute before it really fades after you zone: in your log, 53 of the 60 Puma bars that ran out with no fade had zoned.
- **DoT bars say how many ticks are left** and what the last one did ("3 ticks · 451 a tick"), a bar with a recast cue says when it comes ("cue 12s"), and another player's cast names them on a line of its own under the spell when bars are grouped by target.
- **Experience on the Live page.** The session card says how many kills gave experience, ability points an hour and when the next is due from their spacing ("42 kills, 38 gave experience · 1.3 AA/h · next point in ≈ 40 min"); kills an hour now count the time since your first blow, idle time too, with the rate while fighting beside it.
- **Feed lines for skill-ups, AA points and your death.** "Divination 191", "AA point: 12 unspent", and "You died to a fetid fiend after 0:48 · 1,240 taken in the last 10 s · Scorching Arrow 640".
- **Resists counted.** Spell Timers has a Resisted column (the share of your casts of each spell resisted over the last two weeks), Spell upgrades shows it beside the resist modifier a rank takes off, and "Three resists in a row on {target}" is said once for a target that shrugs off three of your spells running (the words are on Spell Timers › Defaults; blank for silence).
- **Damage meter: more of what the log says.** Your defence counts spells you resist ("You resist a gnoll elite's Burst of Fire!") and a mob's spell that never landed on you; Mend is counted as an ability; a damage shield a magical skin absorbed counts as a miss of it; and the Procs card lists your worn items' exaltations by how often each fired, naming the item beside a proc that fired in the same second.
- **Loot shows what things sell for.** Each row says what it sells for (what auto-loot last sold one for, else what a merchant last offered you for one, your charisma and faction in, else the wiki's value), and each session what its kept loot is worth ("≈ 4p 2g worth").
- **Data Sources shows the lines nothing read.** The chat log's row lists, by shape, the lines no part of the app read (chat left out), with Copy: a game patch that changes a wording shows up there, rather than as timers that quietly stop. main.log's ten-minute performance line counts them too.

- **Hunter: what is up now comes first.** A zone whose named died two minutes ago sinks until it is due back, by your Respawns records; one with a named up (or never seen die) leads.
- **Hunter: time each kill.** With **Time each kill** on (Plan › Hunter), a named still wanted that dies in your zone starts its respawn timer by itself on the Respawns overlay, as long as your log timed it or the tables say, and says "… is up" when it is.
- **Plane of Sky: Targets say whether a mob is up,** from your Respawns records ("died 40 min ago, seen since").
- **Respawns: one stray gap no longer sets the time.** A gap under half the rest (another of the name wandering past) is left out of "≤ 6:00" and said so on hover; a mob the log has not timed shows the spawn tables' time ("~6:40 by the tables"), which the timer editor and "Back in" use too, and the zone's middle timer shows beside it.
- **Factions › Plan: your pace in the zone.** A camp the log has not measured goes at your kills an hour in its zone (from the Slayer history, any zone you have hunted a quarter hour) before the pace assumed for everywhere.
- **Where to go, across your plans.** The Achievements page lists the zones the faction plan you follow, your Slayer plan and your open Hunter achievements want, those more of them want first, with what each wants there and about how long; the achievements overlay shows an **Also here** block when two or more of them want something in the zone you are in (Settings › Overlays can hide it).
- **Progress over time.** Achievements › **Progress over time** shows achievements done a week (from your log), each achievements export against the one before it (done since, Slayer kills, the sections that moved) and the factions moved each day. Each export from now on is kept small to compare with the next.

- **Gear: AC weighed by the AC model.** Past the soft cap, gear AC counts for what one more point is really worth to you (your class's post-cap share, every truncation in), not a flat quarter; and a shield's AC counts for much more, since it lifts the cap too. The Melee role's Offense and haste are worked out from your own numbers (what 1% more damage takes at your Offense and haste), in the same unit as weapon damage.
- **Stats › Combat: from your log.** The crit and hit rates your own melee measured over the last two weeks, the weapon skill your Primary uses and the ATK your gear gives are offered with **Use these**, and **Fill skills from your log** sets each skill your skill-up lines saw higher than what is typed.
- **The merge planner follows the game.** A merge in the log ("… to create a new item: Cloak of Flames +7") or a new inventory export moves the item being planned to its real +N, and Best merge shows worn items at the +N your log saw them merged to since the export. Spell upgrades takes a rank bought with motes as the spell's rank at once.
- **Best merge ranks what the best set would wear,** from the Optimize tab's last run, beside what you wear; the upgrade finder's "At your merge level" says what merging a drop up that far costs and how many of the top mote you can make.
- **Motes › Next.** Best merge and Spell upgrades in one list: each step's cost in Infinitesimal motes' worth, and where it can be said, about how much more of your damage it gives.
- **Motes: where to farm.** Runs has a **By place** table (motes and value an hour for each place), and the planner says where you got the mote you are short of fastest ("The Plane of Hate 4 (Refined) dropped 2.1 Superior an hour over 9 runs").
- **Loot: by mob, and what your recipes need.** **By mob** lists each mob killed in the fights the meter keeps with what dropped and how often a kill; a drop a starred Tradeskills recipe takes says so ("needed by Tumpy Tonic ×10"), and Tradeskills counts what you looted since your inventory export ("+3 since the export").
- **Gear: keep, sell or merge fodder.** Where everything is marks what you wear or the best set would (keep), a second copy of a worn item (merge fodder), and equipment none of your classes can wear that no starred recipe takes (sell).

- **Exploration ticks as you go.** A "Visit Befallen" objective still open is ticked the moment your log shows you entering the zone after your achievements export: on the Achievements page ("visited since your export") and on a tracked Traveler achievement on the overlay, until the next export records it.

- **Optional modules, and the first: Plane of Sky.** Settings › Modules turns on pages for a part of the game not everyone plays. **Plane of Sky** lists every class's Test quests with a chip for each thing a Test takes, how many you hold of how many it needs (counted from your log: looted, less handed in and merged away) and where it drops, the Tests you can hand in first.
- **Plane of Sky: a wish list.** **☆ Want it** keeps a reward on a wish list for the character, and + wants it more than once (two Wu's Fists to merge). It counts the hand-ins your log sees since, and adds up what every wish still takes, item by item, with where each drops. Copies you held before your log began are put right on the Items tab.
- **Plane of Sky: eight tabs.** **Ready** lists the Tests you can hand in now; **Targets** says which mob on which island drops what the Tests shown (or those never handed in, or your wish list) are missing; **Cleanup** lists what you hold that every Test taking it has been handed in for, and says Keep where you hold a whole set for another reward to merge; **Classes** shows each class's Tests handed in and whether it is unlocked; **Ignored** keeps the Tests you never want to see.
- **Plane of Sky: count from your inventory export too.** Items are counted from your log, your `/outputfile inventory` export, or the higher of the two (the default: the export has no currency tab, where the Wind Runes are). A Test counts as handed in from your log, your own +1, its reward in your inventory, or the achievements export saying you obtained it.
- **Plane of Sky: find and sort the Tests.** Filter by class, island and mob, hide the Tests you hold everything for or have handed in, star Tests and show only those, and sort by nearest to done, most recently looted, class or island. A Test opens on each item's two counts, what eqlwiki says of it and the reward, and its hand-ins, which you can put right.

- **Triggers: only when, and the n-th time.** A trigger's **Only when** card limits it to a zone, to fighting or not, to some of your characters, to the n-th match within so many seconds ("the third Gaze in 10 s"), or to a number it caught over or under a value ("{N1} over 300"). A timer's **About** says what its bar is grouped under (its mob) when the phrase's own catch is not it.
- **GINA packages.** Triggers has **Import GINA…** and **Export GINA…**: a .gtp (or its ShareData.xml) comes in with its groups as folders, its text, speech and timers with their ending and ended speech; yours go out the same way.
- **Hear it before you save.** Every speech box on a trigger (the phrase, a timer's warning and its end) and a spell rule's fade has **Hear**.
- **Shipped triggers reach an old profile.** Triggers offers **Add N shipped** for triggers this version ships that your list does not have, the new one first: "Out of mana" is said (once in 30 s) on the game's "Insufficient Mana to cast this spell!".
- **Others' buffs last as long as they did.** Once another player's buff has faded on you twice, its bar runs as long as theirs really last (the middle of the last few), rather than the spell file's length at their level with no focus.
- **The buffs overlay says what is missing.** With group buffs on, a line under the bars lists the buffs your plan still wants ("Missing: Spirit of Wolf, Clarity"), where the reminder that said it was gone in six seconds.
- **Group health: mana, and the tank's own line.** The group health alert can also say a member's low mana ("Aldric is low on mana", off until a line is set) and give named members (the tank) a health line of their own, both on the Triggers page's group health card.
- **Your own mana, said when it runs low.** The Triggers page's group health card has **Say when your own mana is low** (off until switched on; 20% unless changed, said again once you are 10% back over): it reads the Player window's mana bar on the screen, solo or grouped, and works with the group alert off. While a cast bar covers the mana bar nothing is read.
- **Group health with other skins.** A skin whose bars are flat colour in a 1-pixel outline can say how it draws them, colours and bar sizes, in a `groupHealth` section of its `legends-tracker.json` (JuiceboxUI's are the defaults), and the card picks the skin the game shows. **Calibrate from the screen** samples the colours off the Group window (press, then show the game with a member or two in the group), says so aloud and shows them as swatches; **Forget sampled** goes back to the skin's own. The stock skin's textured gauges still cannot be read.
- **Ready-again bars.** A spell of yours with a recast of 30 seconds or more (Harm Touch, Lay on Hands, Divine Aura…) gets a bar on the DoTs & Timers overlay until it can be cast again, from the spell file's recast, and "Harm Touch ready" is said as it ends; a fizzle or an interruption takes it back, and a zone does not end it. On Spell Timers › Defaults, under Ready again; your reuse focus is not taken off.
- **Triggers read lines faster.** A phrase's leading words are looked for before its pattern is run, so a line no trigger could match costs next to nothing however many triggers you keep.

- **What a game update changed in the spells.** When the game rewrites its spell file, the feed says what changed ("12 new spells, 3 of yours changed (Envenomed Bolt…)") and Data Sources' spell-data row opens the whole list, with Copy: the new and gone spells, and for each spell you cast or keep a rule for, its cast, recast, duration, mana, range, effects or messages that moved; a file whose columns moved says so.
- **Save bug report…** on Settings › General writes one zip to attach to an issue: the diagnostics, the app's logs, your settings, the newest crash dump and the last 2,000 lines of your game log, with player and guild names swapped for made-up ones (Kelwyn, Aldric…), what anyone said cut and your Windows user folder taken out.

## 1.6.0 (2026-09-26)

### Spell upgrades: which spells to put motes into

The Motes page has a third tab, **Spell upgrades**. It looks at every spell and song you cast over the last 7, 14 or 30 days of play (or all your logs) and scores the next rank of each, so the best value per upgrade comes first.

- **The rules.** A spell at rank N needs 2^N xp for the next rank, and on a spell every mote counts its xp whatever the rank (Infinitesimal 1, Minor 1, Lesser 2, Potential 4, Major 5 … Infinite 10). There is no tier limit as on items, so the low ranks no item of yours can use any more are worth their full xp here.
- **What a rank gives** comes from the community's EQL spell upgrade (mote) guide, by category: nukes and lifetaps −2% cast, −2% mana, +6% damage; DoTs −4% cast, −2% mana, +5% duration, +3% per tick; heals about +3% healing; debuffs, charms, mezzes and buffs −4% cast, −4% mana, +10% duration; pet summons +1 pet level. Every rank also takes −2% recovery and reuse. Duration bonuses use your Spell Timers' per-rank table.
- **Worth** is casts × points per cast, where a point is one cast made one percent better, weighed the way you choose (damage and healing, duration, mana, cast time, recovery and reuse, a level). Order by worth per xp, worth, or casts; filter by the guide's sections.
- **Pay with** lists the cheapest motes in your stock for the step, lowest rank first, since each rank's combine value doubles while its xp barely grows.
- Spells at rank X are listed last with nothing to plan. Potions, clickies and abilities granted outside the spell book are left out.

### Also

- Spells now carry their mana cost from the spell file.

When a groupmate could give you a buff you want and don't have, it says whom to ask for what — never mid-fight, and at most every ten minutes. Pick the buffs you want from each class's list; out of the box it is each class's best HP & AC, haste, spell haste and mana regen. Songs and buffs under five minutes are left out.
- **Tradeskills page**: star the recipes you make (Distillate of Clarity, Elixir of Greater Concentration, Celestial Healing…) and see each ingredient, how many you have (bags, bank and the tradeskill depot), what to buy, where to get it (vendors, or where it drops, is foraged or crafted), and what a batch costs. Prices are what your log shows you paying last, else the wiki's merchant value, or one you type in. Recipes come from eqlwiki (a one-minute download, refreshed weekly); every recipe and ingredient links to EQ Traders Corner.
- **Pet gear** (Gear › Pet): the best items you own for your pet, within the slots your classes give it. The pet's classes, level and base melee come from its eqlwiki page; what it wears now comes from `/pet inventory check`. Weapons are judged by the melee they give the pet under the Pet Guide's rules.
- **Respawns page**: how long each mob you kill takes to come back, measured from the log, and a one-click timer that starts at every kill of it and counts down on the new Respawns overlay.
- **Charm pets on the damage meter**: a mob charmed by you or a groupmate is that player's pet, apart from other mobs of its name, until it turns on the group or dies.
- **New icon**.
